## Design specificity verdict

The landing is strongly authored for OpenFilm through real workstation proof, photography, exact local-first claims, and the Matte Proof Studio system. The workstation is product-specific in language and recovery behavior, but the dense active rail and pane footers still resemble generic dark photo-management chrome.

The deterministic scan returned zero findings across `src`. Browser inspection found no horizontal overflow or console errors at the inspected desktop size. Mutable overlay injection was blocked by the browser URL policy, so no user-visible detector overlay was claimed.

## Overall impression

The product is already truthful and visually disciplined. The largest opportunity is to make the culling loop feel as quiet as the landing page by keeping immediate review decisions in the rail and moving diagnostics and secondary controls out of that reading path.

## What is working

- Photographs remain the strongest objects on the landing page and in Grid.
- Local Library, Source, Browser Library, and Export boundaries are stated precisely.
- Save, scan, missing-source, recovery, and modal focus states show unusual care.

## Priority issues

### [P1] The Active rail exposes too many simultaneous decisions

Keep Pick, Reject, Rating, and one Selection action immediate. Remove redundant state and move secondary controls into one clear disclosure.

### [P1] Keyboard focus can diverge from Active

Arrow navigation updates `activePhotographId` but may leave DOM focus on the previous Grid cell. Move focus with Active and preserve the roving keyboard path.

### [P1] Disabled controls lack visible requirements

Comparison and 100% zoom explain requirements through title or semantics only. Add short visible requirements without relying on hover.

### [P1] Export lacks an explicit outcome

Add completed, failed, skipped, and remaining counts with a direct retry path for failed entries.

### [P2] The phone-width landing closing is a dead end

Desktop launch links disappear on phones, but the closing section has no replacement boundary or requirements link.

### [P2] Long and repeated status text needs further editing

Remove redundant labels in the active rail and Comparison panes, state unsupported-file truncation, and harden Library names, paths, and errors.
