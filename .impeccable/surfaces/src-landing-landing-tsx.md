## Scope and mode

Production product landing page. Persuade mode. The full browser workstation remains a separate
Operate surface at `app.html`.

## Audience, action, proof, and constraints

The audience is a photographer deciding whether to open a local-first review workstation in a
desktop browser. The primary action opens `app.html`. Proof comes from the real workstation
screenshot, the shipped folder-to-Export workflow, and the precise local-file boundary. There are no
accounts, uploads, analytics, installers, desktop packages, testimonials, or cloud promises.

## Direction

Matte Proof Studio at editorial scale with a restrained kinetic-darkroom composition. A tightly set
Helvetica Neue carries landing headings, while Bodoni remains in the wordmark. The desktop hero
pairs the factual local-first promise with a darkroom photograph, moving film strip, and cropped
workstation proof. Photography remains the strongest material; near-black, warm white, fine rules,
and one sand signal carry the OpenFilm identity.

## Implementation commitments

- The site root is a responsive product story; `app.html` is the browser workstation.
- Desktop launch links open `app.html`. Phone-width layouts replace them with a non-interactive
  Coming soon status.
- A narrow film strip rolls over the hero photograph while visible and pauses offscreen. Reduced
  motion leaves it static, and the hero keeps pointer parallax out.
- Workstation proof crops to the upper controls and photograph row so black stage space does not read
  as empty page space.
- No simulated product state, fake device frame, platform detection, installer, or download route is
  introduced.
