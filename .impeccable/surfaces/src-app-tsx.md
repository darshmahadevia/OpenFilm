## Scope and mode

Production landing state in `App.tsx`. The surface should persuade a casual photographer to begin editing while accurately explaining the product.

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints

Casual photographers deciding whether to open a photograph. The primary action is choosing a local photograph; the secondary action opens the bundled sample. Proof comes from an interactive before-and-after, the actual editing groups, and a plain-language privacy model. No accounts, uploads, invented claims, or destructive edits. The landing yields to the existing editor after a source is imported.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

User-approved prototype B: a darkroom-inspired editorial surface with near-black tones, warm paper, safe-light orange, exposed rules, and a simple circle beside the OpenFilm wordmark. The before-and-after divider is the signature interaction.
